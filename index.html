Cosmo Radio DJ

Personal prototype · Unity 6
You run a radio station in space

  • Audio design
  • Voice
  • Writing
  • Unity dev

A game about hosting a live broadcast between the stars. The player queues songs, keeps the signal clean, and the station plays in-world commercials between tracks. I wrote and voiced the commercials and built the systems that make the broadcast feel live.

In-world radio commercials

  1. Transport Capsules — Intergalactic Airlines0:33
  2. Dick Strange — Schizo Calls0:22

What I built

  • Signal-tuning minigame: the static drifts, and the closer the player holds the frequency, the more the music comes through the noise.
  • Broadcast queue for songs and commercials, with subtitles timed to each ad and loaded from Google Sheets.
  • Simulated live chat and listener count, so the show reacts like a real stream.
  • Audio visualizer and a nebula backdrop that respond to the broadcast.
